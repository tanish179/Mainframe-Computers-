Build a production-ready INTERNAL BUSINESS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM specifically for:

MAINFRAME COMPUTERS
Computer Sales, Services & Repair Business
Kolhapur, Maharashtra, India

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a generic SaaS.
This is NOT a multi-business platform.
This system is ONLY for Mainframe Computers.

The system must be fully usable manually through a separate frontend/dashboard that will be developed independently.

AI/ChatGPT/MCP integration is an OPTIONAL secondary interface.

The business must NEVER depend on AI to operate.

The architecture must allow:
1. Human manually manages the business through the dashboard.
2. ChatGPT/MCP can later read and perform authorized operations using the same backend.
3. Both interfaces use the same business logic and database.

34. MCP RULES
==================================================

AI must NEVER have arbitrary database access.

Never create:

execute_sql()

Every MCP operation must use the same business services as the manual dashboard.

Architecture:

Manual Dashboard
       |
       v
Business Services
       |
       v
Supabase

ChatGPT
       |
       v
MCP
       |
       v
Business Services
       |
       v
Supabase

This ensures AI and humans operate on the same business rules.

==================================================
35. AI SAFETY
==================================================

AI must:

- Never invent financial information.
- Never guess customer balances.
- Never invent stock quantities.
- Never claim an action succeeded unless the backend confirms it.
- Never access unauthorized data.
- Never access another business.
- Never bypass permissions.
- Never expose secrets.
- Never execute arbitrary SQL.

For financial answers, always provide the relevant date range.
