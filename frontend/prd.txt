==================================================
REAL-TIME DATA
==================================================

Dashboard cards and lists must be based on Supabase data.

Examples:

Total Income:

SUM(transactions.amount)
WHERE type = income

Total Expenses:

SUM(transactions.amount)
WHERE type = expense

Net Profit:

income - expenses

Pending Payments:

SUM(payment remaining amounts)

Active Repairs:

COUNT(repairs WHERE status NOT IN delivered)

Low Stock:

COUNT(products WHERE stock_quantity <= minimum_stock)

Do not calculate these from hardcoded demo values in production.
